## 🏆 Featured Projects

### Advanced BVLOS Hybrid VTOL UAV (Patent Pending)
**Role:** Lead Systems Designer  
**Technologies:** Systems Engineering · PX4 · Powertrain Architecture · Aerodynamics · SolidWorks · CFD · Kinematics · Conceptual Design

- Engineered a **2m blended-wing-body (BWB)** UAV in SolidWorks, validating cruise L/D of **13.0–13.7** through a 24-point CFD study and structural core shear safety with **FoS = 2.69**.
- Designed a phase-specific thrust-vectoring propulsion architecture, including propulsion allocation matrices, blade-element stall derating, kinematic torque balancing, and stator thermal constraints.
- Validated actuator mixing and control allocation across VTOL transition phases using **PX4 SITL and Gazebo**.
- Architected onboard avionics, dual-phase thermal management, and BVLOS telemetry with GNSS fallback logic and a ballistic parachute failsafe (**5.4 m/s descent rate**).
- Validated BVLOS mission margins using sim-to-real drag buffers, 10% weather penalties, and 80% battery reserve assumptions.
- Conceptualized rib-segmented BWB packaging for single-person logistics and improved maintainability.
- Achieved projected performance of **8–9 kg payload and 150+ km range**, representing **>2× payload capacity and >1.5× range** compared with comparable 2m-wingspan VTOL platforms.
